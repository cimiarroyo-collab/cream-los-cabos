Photographs communicate the café's atmosphere and menu categories. They are
illustrative and do not claim to show every exact product or customization.
Photographs may be resized and optimized for the app; their provenance is unchanged.

The original site credits [Akimaruri](https://www.akimaruri.com) for its website
experience. Logos, photographs, and the Cream identity remain their owners' work.

The official stylesheet supplies the palette: blue `#293b99`, cream `#edebe3`,
green `#1a9961`, light blue `#a6c4d6`, terracotta `#ab4012`, pink `#f2b0ed`,
and pale yellow `#fadb9c`. The app uses local font fallbacks; Adobe Typekit
font files are not redistributed.

Addresses, opening hours, and general contact numbers in `shared/brand.js` come
from the same official website. Phone numbers are not assigned to an individual
branch because the source does not make that assignment.

## Variantes de aplicación

`logo-cream-azul.svg` reproduce los trazos del wordmark `logo-cream-blanco-01.svg` con el azul oficial `#293B99`. `icon-pato-azul.svg` hace lo mismo con `icon_pato.svg`. No se alteran los trazos ni la forma; son variantes de color para asegurar su legibilidad sobre crema. Los originales blancos se conservan.
